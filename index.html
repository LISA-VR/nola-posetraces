Each trace has 300 poses at 30 fps; pose t is rendered from the Gaussian-splat model of capture frame t, so the scene's motion plays along. Videos cover the frames trained so far. Poses are in the dataset's original COLMAP world with an OpenCV camera (x right, y down, z forward). JSON holds the 4×4 camera-to-world matrix and fx, fy, cx, cy per frame for a 1920×1080 image; CSV holds the camera centre, the camera-to-world quaternion (w, x, y, z) and the intrinsics. Floaters removed = multi-view support (a Gaussian must weigh ≥ 0.5 px in ≥ 4 rig views) and world z in [4, 11].
